# Textbook graphics audit and refactor

29 October 2026 · *The Moving World* · reader: Kym, 12, Mumbai

## The improved prompt

> Act as an educational textbook illustrator, physics visual editor and art director. Audit **every** graphic in this book: chapter diagrams, story illustrations, graphs, live simulations, explorer scenes, cover art, maps, decorative motifs and supporting images.
>
> For each visual, identify the precise idea the reader should understand, the misconception it should prevent, and what the drawing actually makes visible. Assess scientific geometry and quantities, explanatory value, recognisable objects and apparatus, composition, annotation hierarchy, colour semantics, accessibility, phone legibility, theme compatibility and print/offline fallbacks.
>
> Decide whether to retain, refine or redesign it. Give each weak visual a concrete better explanation: an informative cut-away, a sequence, a controlled comparison, a magnified mechanism, linked representations, a measured graph or a useful interaction. Replace basic boxes and labels when a visual mechanism would teach more. Preserve simple abstractions when they already explain well. Added detail must earn its place.
>
> Implement the most consequential improvements, including every verified scientific or readability defect uncovered. Keep the book’s warm paper, restrained ink, personal tone and established force/motion conventions. Use true ratios and directions; label all schematic geometry, exaggerations, sampled motion and simulated measurements. Keep captions, accessible descriptions and supporting prose consistent with the art.
>
> Render and inspect the result. Test the real book on phones and larger screens, in light, sepia and dark, with keyboard access, print, reduced motion and offline operation. Deliver an exhaustive inventory, reviewable before/after examples, a clear account of implemented changes, and specific remaining art opportunities. Do not confuse an exhaustive audit with a need to redraw every good figure.

## Scope and evidence

- **158 registered still figures**, comprising 101 chapter/cover figures and 57 story figures, across 22 drawing modules. Every one was rendered and individually assessed; source-generated contact sheets preserve the starting state and the final result.
- **36 published live widgets**, all three full-page explorers, illustrated chapter navigation, four volume motifs, cover particles, initials medallions and the chapter spine. Three development-only widget fixtures were identified and retained as fixtures.
- The historical map and lifeline graphic, the three existing scale-explorer watercolours, the application icons and sharing image were also inspected. The room, neighbourhood and coast images remain strong reference artwork; their assigned widths are illustrative, not measured cartography. The map’s actual geographical projection, native zoom controls and scene cards work well; future improvement is to simplify pin/label crowding and improve the small lifeline labels, not to decorate the map further.

The complete assessments are in [the chapter review](graphics-chapter-review.md) and [the story/live review](graphics-story-live-review.md). Their per-figure tables describe the starting state and preserve further art opportunities; the implementation record below is the current status.

The repeatable atlas is generated by `source/graphics_audit.py`. It uses the book’s actual Lexend/Literata fonts, browser SVG text measurements (including transforms), a searchable inventory, theme controls and source-module sheets. Browser label metrics are a screening tool; they do not replace inspection for labels crossing rays, apparatus or other drawing marks.

## What changed

The implementation directly revises **57 of the 158 figures**, combining substantive redraws with smaller scientific and layout corrections. It does **not** claim that every opportunity in the exhaustive tables has been implemented.

### Make the drawing do the explaining

- Replaced the formula-only cover with sixteen physical emblems and a four-column layout. Each chapter retains its formula, title and keyboard-operable profile. Repaired all seven missing story destinations; all sixteen now resolve.
- Turned the text-only powers-of-ten ladder into an illustrated logarithmic ruler. Positions retain equal exponent spacing; drawings are explicitly symbols, not common-scale objects.
- Added a magnified ruler endpoint that makes the estimated final digit visible.
- Rebuilt the two reference frames around the same three identifiable bounce events, with gravitational parabolas rather than straight zigzags.
- Drew recognisable local trains, separating platform velocities from the speeds seen by the passenger.
- Replaced generic sliding rectangles with a book and departing hand; kept the backward-force question as a prediction.
- Rebuilt the force/mass comparison with identical wheeled trolleys and countable identical loads. Force and acceleration arrows retain their independent exact ratios.
- Rebuilt Newton’s prism experiment as numbered separation, selection and second-refraction stages. Coloured, named light paths show why selected red stays red.
- Drew the banana’s actual appearance and the corresponding incoming/reflected light; omitted the false outgoing beam when blue is absorbed.
- Rebuilt the motor drawing with unambiguous into/out-of-page symbols, a consistent end-on loop, and correct perpendicular forces and torque.
- Rebuilt fusion bookkeeping to include electrons and neutrinos, with atomic-mass bars and a magnified mass difference.
- Replaced blank spectrum strips with normalised colour spectra and hydrogen lines positioned on a wavelength axis.
- Rebuilt static-charge transfer around a balloon, hair, electron vacancies and a conserved-charge strip.
- Replaced edge-on inverse-square strips with countable face-on patches of areas 1:4:9, plus the linked beam view.
- Replaced the four text-card unit chain with force, lift, clock and electrical-energy examples. The different quantities are distinguished explicitly.
- Rebuilt the terrella field and local needle geometry, and clarified magnetic field lines as direction guides.
- Made the resonance comparison use one common response scale, visible time ticks and separated impulse marks.
- Changed the electricity-duration comparison from logarithmic bar lengths to dots on a labelled logarithmic axis; retained the exact durations.
- Made the Moon’s lit hemisphere visibly lighter in all themes. Improved saturated energy-bar label contrast.

### Correct the physics shown

- Corrected water jets using Bernoulli exit speeds and actual ballistic trajectories to a shared floor: fastest exit does not guarantee greatest range.
- Aligned the sound-pressure wave with particle compression; added correspondence guides.
- Named the translational-only energy-track model an ideal sliding bead and explained the distinction from a rolling marble.
- Equalised the two rocket interaction-force arrows in both chapter and story diagrams.
- Corrected the northward-wire compass deflection, radial Moon-gravity arrow, battery-current placement, EM ladder description and overtone terminology.
- Matched the calcium photon diagram, example and caption at 3.26 eV, with 0.55 eV maximum electron kinetic energy.
- Distinguished a one-solar-mass white dwarf from the Sun’s future remnant mass.
- Replaced an exact-two-eclipses claim with node alignment/eclipse-season wording.
- Replaced rigidly translating Doppler rings with expanding wavefronts centred on fixed emission positions. Kept physical clocks consistent and documented sparse crest sampling and schematic listener offset. Pause/hiding now stops the horn.
- Replaced apparent travelling charges on magnetic field lines with stationary direction marks. Corrected the decorative orbit motion using Kepler’s equation and equal-time swept areas.
- Labelled the generated free-fall trace as simulated; reconciled induction’s physical speed with the drawing scale and aligned Doppler temperatures across its widget and annotation layer.
- Rebuilt mission-explorer orbits with the central body at a focus, true eccentricity, a coherent per-panel scale and explicit resizing notes. Kepler periods are derived from the same orbital radii. Labelled the tide graphic as an ideal model rather than a Haji Ali forecast.
- Corrected story eclipse back-projection, wire-field cross-section and compass scaling, stated the Foucault elapsed time, and separated cooling to 0°C from complete freezing. Marked the Moon-departure gap as enlarged and distinguished acceleration from speed.

### Make detail accessible

Every chapter/story still figure has a **Look closely** button. It opens the original vector drawing at native width, with phone scrolling, a visible scroll hint and a Fit control. Native modal focus, Escape, exact opener focus restoration, nested story dialogs, unique SVG clip/pattern/gradient definitions and print exclusion were exercised in the actual book. Ordinary reading retains the full composition. Figures remain available without JavaScript and in print.

Detected clipping and label collisions were corrected by moving, splitting or reflowing labels rather than shrinking their type. The final atlas has zero flagged labels outside the viewBox and zero flagged text-to-text collisions across all 158 figures. Browser measurements do not certify every artistic judgement or every possible live-widget state.

These are factual checks; the new drawings are original programmatic SVGs. Chapter 15’s grounding and provenance ledger record the newly explicit wavelength and mass values.
