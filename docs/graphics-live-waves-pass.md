# Live waves, light, field and sky graphics pass

Scope: all 20 assigned rows in `docs/graphics-story-live-review.md`. The aim was to make each visual explain a mechanism, keep evidence distinguishable from a model, and make small drawn controls available as real controls. Existing physical models, deliberate sensor/audio activation and the three-primary-control structure are retained. Native details hold secondary explanations or fine adjustments.

| Widget | Implemented | Retained / model boundary |
|---|---|---|
| circuit | Separate Circuit, Energy per charge and Rope views; full-size component symbols, aligned A/B/C charge-rate ledger and readable energy transfers. Below threshold the energy view explicitly withholds individual LED voltages that this model cannot resolve. | Approximate 2 V LED model; current and power conservation checks. Conventional-charge markers are not individual electrons. Rope contact analogy is explicitly limited. |
| colourmix | Recognisable banana, leaf, folded cloth and kurta silhouettes; equivalent labelled native object select synchronises with canvas cards. | Additive/subtractive modes and original RGB calculations; three-channel reflectance is identified as an idealisation. |

| expansion | Reference and stretched wavelength traces share an x scale alongside the balloon, separating wavelength from display colour. Observer-centred distance comparison remains. | Fixed-size ants and galaxies; balloon is explicitly a projection/analogy and has no privileged cosmic centre. |
