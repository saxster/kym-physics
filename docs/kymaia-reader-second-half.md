## Three-sentence explain-back

A cold-feeling metal plate can be at the same temperature as wood because it takes energy from my hand faster. A wire does not use up charge: the battery transfers energy around a closed loop, and no dry-hand calculation makes mains safe to touch. When a picture or experiment is a model, I should compare what it predicts with what I really see: ink dots grow on a balloon, but that is a limit of the picture of galaxies.
