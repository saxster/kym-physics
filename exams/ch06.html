Independent practice · 3 suggested marks

A uniform normal force of 72 N acts over 0.024 m². Calculate the pressure.

Model answer and marking guidance

Original question; the book’s suggested marking guidance. Equivalent scientifically correct wording is welcome.

  1. Pressure = normal force ÷ area.
  2. 72 ÷ 0.024 = 3000 Pa.
  3. The answer is 3000 Pa.
Why these points earn credit
  1. Pressure = normal force ÷ area. [1]
  2. Correct substitution: 72 ÷ 0.024. [1]
  3. The answer is 3000 Pa. [1]

Accept equivalent scientifically correct wording. For calculations, give credit for a correct relationship and correctly substituted values even if a later arithmetic slip occurs.

A common difficulty: A larger total force need not mean a larger pressure if the area also changes.
