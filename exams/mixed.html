Back at the starting gate

Independent practice · 3 suggested marks

A child walks 120 m east along a straight path, then 120 m west to the starting gate. The whole trip takes 160 s. Calculate (a) total distance, (b) displacement, and (c) average speed.

Model answer and marking guidance

Original question; the book’s suggested marking guidance. Equivalent scientifically correct wording is welcome.

  1. (a) Total distance = 240 m.
  2. (b) Displacement = 0 m.
  3. (c) Average speed = 1.5 m/s. The zero displacement does not make the distance or average speed zero.
Why these points earn credit
  1. Total distance is 120 + 120 = 240 m. [1]
  2. Displacement is zero because the final position equals the starting position. [1]
  3. Average speed is 240 ÷ 160 = 1.5 m/s. [1]

Equivalent correct scientific wording is accepted; the listed points explain what earns credit.

A common difficulty: Using zero displacement in the speed equation would calculate average velocity, not average speed.
