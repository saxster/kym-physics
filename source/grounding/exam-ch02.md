## Worked/guided audit

- `ch02-worked-explanation`: Speed = 90 ÷ 18 = 5 m/s. Constant velocity means zero acceleration. The horizontal resultant force is therefore zero.
- `ch02-guided-explanation`: Friction from the floor acts right, opposing the relative sliding. If this is the only horizontal force, the resultant is right while the box moves left. Its leftward speed therefore decreases.
