## Rotational equilibrium and stability source extension

The independent reviewer opened and verified these primary sources for the added core coverage:

- OpenStax *College Physics 2e*, 9.2, https://openstax.org/books/college-physics-2e/pages/9-2-the-second-condition-for-equilibrium : moment uses the perpendicular lever arm; rotational equilibrium balances clockwise and anticlockwise moments.
- OpenStax *College Physics 2e*, 9.3, https://openstax.org/books/college-physics-2e/pages/9-3-stability : a restoring moment and the line of weight relative to the support base explain stability; lowering the centre of gravity and widening the base generally increase the limiting tilt.

The plumb-line method is already described in the book's Chapter 3.1. Its physical justification is rotational equilibrium: a freely suspended shape settles with its weight line through the suspension pivot; verticals from two distinct suspension points therefore intersect at the centre of gravity. The task is a written method, not an instruction to handle a sharp pin unsupervised. No source diagram or source question was copied.
