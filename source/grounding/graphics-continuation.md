# Graphics fidelity continuation — 29 October 2026

This pass retains the chapter grounding and revises original SVG/canvas explanations. It introduces no third-party art.

- **Kibble balance:** NIST’s [measurement principle](https://www.nist.gov/si-redefinition/kilogram/kilogram-kibble-balance) separates weighing (`mg=BLI`) from velocity (`U=BLv`) mode. Combining them yields `mgv=UI`; modes are not simultaneous. The revised story provenance records this source.
- **Eye:** [OpenStax College Physics 2e 26.1](https://openstax.org/books/college-physics-2e/pages/26-1-physics-of-the-eye) supports the stronger corneal contribution and adjustable crystalline lens. The SVG uses two paraxial surfaces with a common inverted image plane; the live view instead uses an explicitly effective single thin lens, a 2.00 cm retina plane and `25×400^(slider/100)` cm object mapping. Both are explanatory models, not measured anatomy.
- **Interference:** [OpenStax 27.3](https://openstax.org/books/college-physics-2e/pages/27-3-youngs-double-slit-experiment) supplies integer/half-integer wavelength path conditions. The static intensity is computed from both actual path lengths with equal-amplitude `cos²(πΔr/λ)`. Source arcs have the same wavelength. Finite slit width and its envelope are omitted.
- **Proton landmark:** CERN’s [quark teaching reference](https://educational-resources.web.cern.ch/sites/default/files/documents/Quark%20Puzzle%20Scaffolded%20Worksheet.pdf) supports the symbolic up/up/down composition. The marks are explicitly neither hard balls nor orbital trajectories. The electron density rendering likewise describes a schematic cloud, not an electron path or a quantitatively sampled wavefunction.
- **Aditya-L1:** ISRO describes a [three-dimensional halo orbit](https://www.isro.gov.in/halo-orbit-insertion-adtya-l1.html) and distinguishes the [XY projection](https://www.isro.gov.in/Aditya_L1_Mission_Completion_of_First_Halo_Orbit.html) from the trajectory. The new oblique view is explicitly schematic; it does not claim an ephemeris reconstruction. L1 has no mass.
- **Authored numerical models:** All shared numerical strips now trace to the model actually used by their widget. Examples checked independently: 9 V with three approximate 2 V LEDs leaves 3 V across 330 Ω, giving 9.09 mA; a 0.60 kg cart at +2 m/s plus 0.30 kg at −0.5 m/s stick at +1.167 m/s; hollowing preserves mass while increasing outer volume by ten; pressure displays convert pascals to kPa; mercury’s offered density is 13,600 kg/m³. The photon, force, thermal and sensor model limitations remain visible.
- **Scales and limits:** 500 nm / 0.1 nm = 5,000. One AU is the mean Earth–Sun orbital radius, not the orbit’s diameter. The Rayleigh comparison `(700/450)^4 ≈ 5.86` uses the widget’s wavelengths. Video fps and physical calibration are separate; calibrating a real clip cannot change gravity. A cannon path reaching 4.2 Earth radii is a drawing horizon, while the sign of specific orbital energy determines escape.
