Written by the chapter writer for the widget agents. No `:::live` lines are in `src/ch05.md`; add them under the
figures named below when the widgets exist. Every widget: book colours, labelled forces and vectors, no autoplay,

## w_cannon3d — 5.2 Newton's cannon (three.js)

- **Figure:** `c5_cannon` (still: five computed shots from a mountain top).
- **Teaches:** an orbit is falling and missing; the circular speed near Earth is about 8 km/s and escape about
  11 km/s; mass does not matter.
- **Controls (3):**
  1. *Speed* slider, 0 to 12 km/s in 0.1 km/s steps.
  2. *Fire* button (a second press clears the trail and fires again; up to three trails stay on screen).
  3. Drag to turn the globe (three_kit's turn buttons; gentle spin while playing).
- **Readout:** for each shot: "lands after … km along the ground" / "orbit: back to the cannon in … minutes, lowest
  point … km, highest … km" / "escapes: never comes back"; the current altitude and speed while in flight; and a
  standing line "no air in this model; the mountain is drawn far too tall". When the speed is set within 0.1 km/s
  of 7.9 km/s the readout says "about 8 km/s: a circle"; above 11.2 km/s, "about 11 km/s: escape".
- **Physics to honour:** GM = 3.986 × 10¹⁴ m³/s² (G and Earth's mass from the 8.01SC data sheet), R = 6371 km;
  launch from a mountain drawn at about 3% of R but computed from R + 10 km so the circular speed is 7.9 km/s and
  escape 11.2 km/s; integrate with RK4 or a symplectic step; check specific energy ½v² − GM/r each frame and keep
  its drift under 0.1% per orbit (readout shows "energy check: ok"); a shot that reaches r < R lands. Trajectory
  shape must match the still's five cases at 0.55, 0.82, 1.0, 1.17 and 1.46 times the circular speed.
- **Slowed or exaggerated:** time sped up (one real second = about 2 minutes of flight; readout says so); the
  mountain height exaggerated; Earth textured in book colours, no photographs.
- **Sensors/audio:** none. WebGL fallback: the still stays.

## w_freefall — 5.2 The phone that reads zero (sensor lab)

- **Figure:** `c5_phone_drop` (still: the sensor trace of a 40 cm drop).
- **Teaches:** the sensor reads 9.8 while something holds the phone up and about 0 while it falls; weight unchanged,
  the feeling of weight gone.
- **Controls (3):**
  1. The *Use the motion sensor* button that `api.motion()` provides (iOS permission on that press).
  2. *Clear* button (empties the trace).
  3. *Replay the recorded drop* button, which plays the still's schematic trace through the same graph for readers
     without a sensor, labelled "recorded example, not your phone".
- **Readout:** live magnitude |a| in m/s² ("9.8: the table is holding the phone up"); when |a| stays below 2 m/s²
  for at least 0.1 s and then jumps above 12 m/s²: "that was … s of free fall. For that time nothing pushed on the
  phone, so it felt no weight. From the time, the drop was about … cm (½ g t²)". If the drop lasts more than 0.6 s
  (about 1.8 m) say "that was a long drop; please drop onto a pillow from no higher than your knee".
- **Physics to honour:** plot |a| = √(x² + y² + z²) from `api.motion`'s samples against time, 4 s window scrolling;
  dashed reference line at 9.8; free-fall detection as above; height from ½ · 9.8 · t² and shown as "about", since
  the sensor's sample rate limits the timing. Nothing leaves the device (api.motion already says so).
- **Slowed or exaggerated:** nothing; raw sensor values. The replay trace is schematic and says so.
- **Sensors/audio:** `api.motion()`; without a sensor the API points to phyphox and the replay button still works.
  Safety line on the stage: "a soft pillow on the floor, no higher than your knee".
