# Discovery mechanics: grounding, models and provenance

15 November 2026. This note covers only `live/discovery_mechanics.mjs` and its focused unit test. Ten activities deepen existing lesson IDs; they do not add a second catalogue. Text, UI wording, geometry and canvas art are original implementations for Kym, age twelve in Mumbai. All observations are labelled simulations by the parent discovery workspace. No audio, animation timer, sensor request, remote fetch or autoplay exists in this module.

Design inspiration comes from recommendations 1, 2, 7, 8, 10–14, 23 and 28 in [the existing Dr Jones research](../../docs/drjones-apps-recommendations.md). Only general interaction ideas were used: coordinated representations, authored stages, error comparisons, reading instruments, suspension tests, controlled stopping comparisons, target missions, collision inference, support readings, density inference and Earth-size geometry. No Dr Jones code, exact prose, artwork, app data or undocumented numerical model was copied. The research distinguishes directly inspected interfaces from catalogue inspiration; this implementation makes no new claims about those apps or measured learning effectiveness.

## Explicit models and assumptions

All local examples are chosen model inputs, not recorded measurements of Mumbai, a real train, road or ancient experiment. Gravity is the existing engine constant `G = 9.8 m/s²`.

- **Train:** velocity is linearly joined through `(0,0)`, `(4,v1)`, `(8,v2)`, `(12,v3)` in seconds and m/s. Each stage integrates `constantAcceleration`; displacement and velocity remain continuous at joins, while acceleration can jump. The same selected time drives all readouts, scene and cursor. Reverse velocity is allowed and produces signed displacement. Editable graph handles and sliders set the same three values. For velocities 8, 8, 0 m/s, the areas are 16 + 32 + 16 = **64 m**. At t = 2 s, x = **4 m**, v = **4 m/s**, a = **2 m/s²**.
- **Repeated readings:** reuse `measurement`, including its fixed five offsets `[-0.8, 0.3, -0.1, 0.8, -0.2]`, scaled by the disturbance size, shared zero offset and rounding to the selected division. These offsets sum to zero before rounding. They are deterministic illustrative variations, not independent random samples or an uncertainty estimate. With a 40 g reference, 0.1 g divisions, scatter 1 g and +3 g bias, the mean error is **+3 g**. Reducing scatter cannot remove a common bias.
- **Ruler:** both endpoints snap to the chosen marked divisions. Length is endpoint difference; translating both endpoints leaves it unchanged. A pencil spanning **15 to 60 mm** has length **45 mm**, also when moved to 25–70 mm. The visual ruler is explicitly millimetres, not centimetres. Its challenge hides endpoint-position and length controls; labelled divisions provide evidence.
- **Polygon:** vertices are in millimetres, counterclockwise in a mathematical x-right/y-up plane. Uniform area density makes the centre the area centroid. For consecutive vertices, define `q = x_i y_(i+1) − x_(i+1) y_i`; then `A = abs(sum(q))/2`, `cx = sum((x_i+x_(i+1))q)/(3 sum(q))`, and similarly for y. These follow by summing signed triangular areas and first moments about the origin. A triangle `(0,0),(6,0),(0,3)` has area **9 mm²** and centre **(2,1) mm**; reversing its order gives the same centre. Presets and positive radial vertices in angle order prevent self-intersections and degenerate areas. A keyboard user can choose a preset, edit any original vertex radius, add a vertex by angle/radius and set the guessed x/y location. Pointer insertion sets the same added-vertex controls. Two independent pin selections rotate the sheet so the centre lies vertically below the selected pin. Dashed lines are also traced back into the flat coordinates; their crossing supplies measurement evidence. Coincident or near-parallel suspension lines are flagged. This is a constrained polygon editor, not arbitrary freehand or holes.
- **Stopping:** `v = speed_kmh / 3.6`, reaction distance `v t_reaction`, braking acceleration `−μg` and braking distance `v²/(2μg)`. Reuses `slide` for the braking stage. Baseline and changed car use this identical model. At 36 km/h, 1 s and μ = 0.5, thinking distance is **10 m**, braking distance **10.2040816 m**, total **20.2040816 m**. Doubling speed multiplies the two distances by two and four respectively. μ is deliberately a numerical grip assumption, not a claimed coefficient for a particular road, weather or tyre. The model does not predict real safe distances.
- **Projectile:** reuse the existing horizontal-launch `projectile`. `t_fall = sqrt(2h/g)` and `x = vx t`, with no air resistance. A shelf **1.225 m** high gives a **0.5 s** fall; a **4 m/s** launch reaches **2 m** sideways. The time control is a fraction from zero to landing; it does not autoplay. The basket’s ±0.25 m opening is a chosen design tolerance. The challenge asks for the required sideways speed, with that computed reading and speed control hidden.
- **Collision:** perfectly sticking one-dimensional trolleys, no external horizontal impulse. `v_final = (m1 u1 + m2 u2)/(m1+m2)`. Show initial and final kinetic energy separately; their difference is conversion to unmodelled internal energy/sound. At **2 kg, +4 m/s** and **1 kg, −2 m/s**, total momentum is **6 kg m/s**, shared velocity **2 m/s**, energies **18 J → 6 J**, converted **12 J**. Rewind shows an ideal instantaneous join, not resolved contact mechanics. The challenge hides the first approach-velocity control and initial/converted-energy readings; masses, second velocity and final motion remain usable evidence.
- **Bridge:** rigid uniform plank of mass M and support separation L, point load m at x in `[0,L]`. Summing force gives `R_left + R_right = (M+m)g`; torque about the left gives `R_right L = MgL/2 + mgx`. Therefore `R_right = g(M/2 + mx/L)`. For **L = 4 m, M = 10 kg, m = 40 kg, x = 0**, the readings are **441 N left, 49 N right**. Both remain nonnegative throughout the allowed load range. This does not model bridge bending, dynamics or structural strength.
- **Density:** mass input is g and sealed external volume is cm³; both convert to SI before reusing `buoyancy`. The balance, displacement tank, fully immersed force meter and free release are selectable evidence stations. At full immersion, `F_holding = mg − rho_water g V`, signed upward; a negative result means a downward restraint is needed. The release model floats with fraction `rho_object/rho_water` below water when less than one, otherwise sinks or is neutral. **200 g / 100 cm³ = 2000 kg/m³**, with weight **1.96 N** and submerged upward holding force **0.98 N**. **100 g / 200 cm³ = 500 kg/m³**, floating half submerged. Density is an average, not unique proof of a material. A sealed hollow outer volume can change while mass stays fixed.
- **Earth:** reuse `earthMeasure(distance_km,angle_deg,1000)`; one input distance unit is one kilometre, so no ancient unit conversion is implied. `C_km = distance_km × 360 / angle_deg`. Shadow length is `stick_height × tan(angle)`. A model **800 km** arc and **7.2°** angle give **40,000 km**. The separate spherical reference uses the existing **6371 km** radius, hence **40,030.1736 km**. The Earth drawing enlarges the central angle for legibility and labels that enlargement; the stick inset uses the actual angle. Same-meridian, simultaneous-noon, vertical-stick, spherical-Earth and parallel-ray assumptions are stated. No precise historical measurement is attributed to Eratosthenes.

## Verification and boundaries

The focused command is `node source/tests/test_discovery_mechanics.mjs`. It checks deterministic seeds and changed selected values, all control options/endpoints plus simultaneous minimums and maximums for four seeds, finite nested compute outputs, pure computation, source URL shape, existing catalogue lesson IDs, challenge-hidden-field contracts, and canvas API calls with finite arguments. It also checks independent known examples, stage continuity, signed motion, momentum/energy accounts, bridge force/torque balances, and pointer–keyboard equivalence.

Actual result on 15 November: **10 activities, 464 seeded control-boundary/draw cases, all analytic/conservation/pointer/challenge-contract checks passed**. Node reports its existing module-type warning when importing `investigation_draw.js` from this `.mjs` test; no package configuration was changed. Canvas calls are a numerical smoke check, not visual verification. The root task owns rendered UI review, challenge reveal interactions, retention/comparison, notebook export, bundling and the final local gate. This module did not run browsers, builds, a broad suite or hosted CI, and made no commit.

Remaining scope limits: the polygon editor permits presets, spoke edits and one added vertex, not unrestricted freehand silhouettes or holes; projectile targeting is a horizontal shelf launch, not arbitrary launch angles or alternate planets; error readings are illustrative rather than stochastic. These are deliberate bounded implementations with their assumptions exposed, not claimed complete reproductions of the inspiration apps.
