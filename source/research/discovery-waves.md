## Independent calculation references

The following values were separately recomputed with Python `math` and elementary arithmetic before settling the tests; tests store fixed numerical expectations rather than obtaining their expected values from the production models.

* Equal 3 m paths, `c=299792458 m/s`: air 10.00692285594456 ns; wave-water 13.339228166974099 ns; rival corpuscular-water 7.507068909185717 ns. Incident 45° gives refracted angle about 32.036723°.
* One gap 2 cm wide, wavelength 1 cm, screen 2 m away: at transverse screen position 1 m, `sinc²(π·2/√5) = 0.013428602617085762`. Incoming speed 6 cm/s gives 6 Hz. The marked piece at mean position 5 cm starts at +3 mm, reaches −3 mm after 1/12 s, and returns after 1/6 s. Reflection sums cancel exactly at the 10 cm end. A 1.25 cm incoming wavelength at 6 cm/s gives 4.8 Hz; the slower region has wavelength 0.75 cm and speed 3.6 cm/s, still 4.8 Hz.
* A 300 Hz horn travelling at 30 m/s in 348.7 m/s still air gives front frequency 328.2397238782554 Hz, rear 276.234486400845 Hz, driver 300 Hz. Front spacing 1.0623333333333334 m; rear 1.2623333333333333 m.
* A 1000 mm objective and 20 mm eyepiece give 50× and approximately 1° field for the stipulated 50° apparent field. A 100 mm aperture at 550 nm gives 1.3840368499180165 arcseconds; doubling aperture halves this limit.
* 200 W for 1000 s supplies 200 kJ. At 20 V, the current is 10 A. After 42 kJ warms the initial ice, 158 kJ has melted `158/334` of the kilogram; temperature remains 0 °C. At total 1000 kJ the boiling fraction is `205.4/2256` and temperature is 100 °C.
