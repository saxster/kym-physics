# Source inventory and licence facts for Kym's story-first physics textbook

Checked 2026-10-28. Every URL below is one I fetched or saw in search results. Anything I could not confirm is marked **UNVERIFIED**. The web-search budget ran out partway through section 8, so a few history items are thinner than the rest.

**Two findings change the plan:**
1. **OpenStax has moved to CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.** Every OpenStax book page I fetched now says this; it is no longer CC BY. It still fits our licence, but the attribution rules are stricter and there is a new clause about AI (see 3).
2. **PhET's licence changed on 29 March 2026.** Only simulations released before that date are confirmed as CC BY 4.0 (see 4).

Per-item "© … excluded from our Creative Commons license" notices inside OCW files are a known OCW practice, but **UNVERIFIED** in this pass. Check each figure before reusing it.

## 2. Walter Lewin

- **MIT's announcement** (8 Dec 2014): "MIT indefinitely removes online physics lectures and courses by Walter Lewin" — https://news.mit.edu/2014/lewin-courses-removed-1208. It removed his lectures from OCW and his MITx courses from edX, "following a determination that Dr. Lewin engaged in online sexual harassment in violation of MIT policies".
- **News report:** The Tech, 9 Dec 2014 — https://thetech.com/2014/12/09/walterlewin-v134-n60. It adds that his emeritus title was revoked and the OCW YouTube channel was included.
- **Still listed anywhere?** 8.01L (2005) and both 8.02 versions above do not list him. One mirror (https://opencw.aprende.org/courses/physics/8-01sc-physics-i-classical-mechanics-fall-2010) credits an "8.01SC Fall 2010" to Lewin, Dourmashkin and others. Whether that exists on ocw.mit.edu today is **UNVERIFIED**.
- **Rule for us:** never use the 1999 Lewin videos (archive.org copies) or Fall 2010 materials.

**Attribution rule.** Digital adaptations must carry the same CC BY-NC-SA licence and show "Access for free at https://openstax.org/books/…" **on every page view**.

**AI clause — read this one.** The prefaces say the books may not be "used to train large language models or be ingested into generative AI offerings without OpenStax's prior written permission." Our workflow has an AI reading OpenStax and re-teaching it. That probably counts as ingestion, so it needs a human decision before we start. Options are to ask OpenStax for permission, or to have a human-written summary step in between.

**Third-party figures.** Credits vary by figure. Examples: College Physics 2e Fig 1.1 is "credit: NASA, ESA, Hubble Heritage, and the Digitized Sky Survey 2"; Physics Fig 4.1 is "(Credit: Jin Jang)". OpenStax says only images *it holds copyright in* are under CC BY-NC-SA. Check every figure's credit line before reusing it, or redraw it ourselves.

## 4. PhET (University of Colorado Boulder)

- **The licence** (https://phet.colorado.edu/en/licensing/html): "Historical PhET Simulations were published under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 License". Historical means released before **29 March 2026**; the About dialog shows a sim's date. The agreement explicitly does **not** cover Regular HTML sims published after that date, PhET-iO, or the Java/Flash legacy sims. **The licence for post-cutoff sims is UNVERIFIED.** One search summary mentioned a move to CC BY-NC, but I could not find the source.
- **Attribution must read:** "Simulation by PhET Interactive Simulations, University of Colorado Boulder, licensed under CC BY 4.0", plus https://phet.colorado.edu. It must sit next to the sim. Translation is allowed; "other modifications are not permitted"; the PhET logo must stay visible.
- **Older Java/Flash sims** were CC BY 3.0 US or GPL (https://phet.colorado.edu/en/licensing/java).
- **Teacher activities:** submissions must be under an open licence, and CC BY 4.0 qualifies (https://phet.colorado.edu/mk/licensing/activity).
- **Plan:** link out to the sims, or embed one with the attribution next to it. Each chapter can say "go play with this".

## 6. Feynman, NASA, ISRO, ESA

- **Feynman Lectures** (https://www.feynmanlectures.caltech.edu/). The site returned 403. Search results report it as © California Institute of Technology, free "to read, look at, and listen to online", with no right to download. Coverage: [APS News 2014](https://www.aps.org/apsnews/2014/10/feynman-lectures-now-freely-available-online). **Link and cite only.**
- **NASA** (https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/). Media "generally are not subject to copyright in the United States", and "NASA should be acknowledged as the source." The insignia and logos are "not in the public domain." Third-party material on NASA sites needs the rights holder's permission. **Reusable with credit**, after checking each image's credit line.
- **ISRO** (https://www.isro.gov.in/Copyright_Policy.html). Material "may be reproduced free of charge in any format or media without requiring specific permission". It must be "reproduced accurately and not being used in a derogatory manner or in a misleading context", and "the source must be prominently acknowledged". Material marked as third-party copyright is excluded. **So Chandrayaan-3 and Aditya-L1 images are usable unaltered, with credit, and marked as outside our CC licence.** NRSC satellite imagery has stricter terms (https://www.nrsc.gov.in/nrscnew/termsConditions.php).
- **ESA** (https://www.esa.int/Services/Creative_Commons_Attribution-ShareAlike_3.0_IGO_CC_BY-SA_3.0_IGO_licence). CC BY-SA 3.0 IGO applies only "where expressly stated". Credit format: "ESA/… CC BY-SA 3.0 IGO". Images without that statement fall under ESA's standard terms (https://www.esa.int/spaceinimages/ESA_Multimedia/Copyright_Notice_Images, not fetched). Per a search summary of ESA's pages, non-commercial use is free with credit. **UNVERIFIED** in detail.

## (a) Licence verdict

**Can be adapted** (re-taught, with figures remixed where allowed, under our CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, with attribution and a note of what we changed):
- **MIT OCW** courses (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0). No MIT logo, no implied endorsement. Check each figure for third-party notices.
- **OpenStax** books (now CC BY-NC-SA 4.0). Put "Access for free at …" on every page and check each figure's credit. The AI clause needs a decision first (see 3).
- **PhET** sims from before 29 March 2026 (CC BY 4.0), but as unmodified sims with their attribution, linked or embedded. Teacher activities under CC BY 4.0 can be adapted.
- **NASA** images (credit NASA).

**Usable but kept outside our licence**, labelled with their own terms:
- **ISRO** images (unaltered, source prominently credited).
- **ESA** images: BY-SA IGO ones and standard-terms ones.
